Regulatory Map

The packages in this monorepo are designed to conform to specific regional and global legal standards for eSignatures, data privacy, and encryption export regulations.

Choose a target framework to see which packages satisfy its requirements.

  • 🛡️

    Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 / GDPR

    Requires total removal of PII data. Visual blackouts leak data, whereas our vector sanitizer strips the characters completely from page streams.

    @dottedice/pdf-vector-sanitizer @dottedice/pdf-redaction-engine
  • ⚖️

    BSA 2023 / Indian Evidence Act Section 65B

    Admissibility of electronic records requires audit trails showing document integrity and a declaration from the system admin.

    @dottedice/esign-audit-verifier @dottedice/section-65b-certificate
  • 🌐

    eIDAS (Regulation EU 910/2014) / PAdES

    Standardizes European digital signatures. Detached signatures must map to specific PDF byte ranges and support LTV/TSA hooks.

    @dottedice/pades-pdf-signer @dottedice/cms-signed-data @dottedice/timestamp-authority-client @dottedice/trust-anchor-resolver
  • 🔒

    US EAR Export Controls (ECCN 5D002)

    Our source code is publicly accessible and qualifies for the TSU export exemption (15 CFR 740.13(e)).

Document Processing Pipeline

Tracing the lifecycle of a document from raw input to cryptographically verified, compliant output.

1
Sanitize @dottedice/pdf-vector-sanitizer removes target text characters from PDF contents stream.
2
Sign @dottedice/pades-pdf-signer allocates Sig dictionary and writes bytes.
3
Validate @dottedice/cms-signature-validator cryptographically verifies signature envelopes.
4
Resolve @dottedice/trust-anchor-resolver verifies cert chains against root anchors.
